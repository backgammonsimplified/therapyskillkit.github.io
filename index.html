ABOUT THIS KIT

Education, not diagnosis

Therapy Skill Kit is a self-guided educational resource. It can help you understand and practice common CBT, DBT, mindfulness, goal-setting, and wellness skills, but it is not a substitute for professional mental health care.

If you are in immediate danger or may act on thoughts of suicide or self-harm, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.
