HOW TO USE IT

A starting point, not a diagnosis

The finder does not evaluate mental health conditions. It simply matches your selections and words to relevant lessons in this site.

If you are in immediate danger or may act on thoughts of suicide or self-harm, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.
